2

Pick your AI

Then right-click any email.

How it works

Web AIs

Pick "Ask ChatGPT" or another web AI. The extension copies the thread and opens the site next to Gmail. Press Cmd+V (Ctrl+V), then Enter.

Desktop agents

Pick "Open in Claude Code" or "Open in Codex (GPT)". A session opens on your computer and reads the thread through its own Gmail connection.

Copy for any AI

Copies the same email text and opens nothing, so you can paste it wherever you like.

Your AIs appear at the top of Gmail's right-click menu on inbox rows, and in Chrome's right-click menu inside an open thread (grouped under "Open in AI Agent for Gmail" when you pick more than one). Click the toolbar icon to change them.

Safe by default

Nothing sent to us

Runs on your computer. No server, no analytics, no tracking. It stores only your settings.

Reads only what you pick

It reads an email only after you choose an AI for it, and only that thread.

You press send

Web AIs get the email only from your clipboard. You paste it, check it and press Enter yourself.

No hidden instructions

Desktop agents never see the subject or body in their prompt. Web prompts mark the email as information, not instructions.

What the AI receives

Web AIs and Copy for any AI

Long threads are cut at 6,000 characters per message and 30,000 in all, marked [truncated]. Attachments are not included.

Tell me where it stands and what is being asked of me, check the relevant source systems, then recommend the next step. Draft replies only; send nothing without my approval.

No subject or body: the agent reads the thread through its own Gmail connection.

Install from the download

  1. Download the zip and unzip it somewhere it will stay.
  2. Open chrome://extensions, switch on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.
  3. On the settings page that opens, pick your AI.
  4. Reload Gmail and right-click an email. For Claude Code or Codex, tick Always allow the first time.

Web AIs need only Chrome. Claude Code and Codex need the Claude desktop app or OpenAI's ChatGPT desktop app with Codex, with Gmail connected for the same mailbox. Tested on macOS.
