Open in AI Agent for Gmail
All browsers · GitHub (MIT)

Open Gmail threads in ChatGPT or Codex with one right-click.

Skip copy-pasting collapsed messages one by one, and skip SaaS wrappers that store your inbox on third-party servers. Open in AI Agent reads the full thread inside your browser and hands it straight to your own chatgpt.com session or the Codex desktop app.

Add to Chrome (Free) Edge, Brave, Arc & Firefox

Version 1.6.5 · MIT open source · 100% local in your browser.

Built for real inbox work

  • Separate chats for multiple threads: Enable optional composer auto-fill in Settings, tick 2 to 5 emails in Gmail, and open each thread in its own pre-filled ChatGPT tab without sending anything until you review and press Enter.
  • Batch triage up to 25 unread emails: Use Triage unread in Gmail's toolbar to classify unread mail into Action, Consolidate, Disregard and Draft without marking anything read.
  • Safe Codex handoff: When opening in Codex on your desktop, the link carries only your mailbox address, thread ID and Gmail link (never untrusted email text), with a read-only guard until you reply.
